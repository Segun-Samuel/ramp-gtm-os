## Positioning statement (INFERRED synthesis)

For finance teams at growing companies that run cards, expenses and bills across separate tools, Ramp is the finance operations platform that builds control into every card swipe, invoice and reimbursement and closes the books automatically. Unlike a bank card paired with Expensify, Concur or Bill.com, Ramp does it on one platform, with agents that review every expense and code every transaction, and a switch that takes days, not months. More than 85,000 businesses use it, and Ramp says its median customer saves 7.5%.

## Caveats

- Every number above is Ramp's own claim on a Ramp page or release.
- The switcher counts come from Ramp's internal Salesforce records, and Ramp's own pages disagree on some (Brex: 5,500+ on the versus page, 4,200+ on the switcher post; Amex: 18,000+ and 11,500+ on one page) [cmp 3, cus 1.3].
- 7.5% is a median, and Ramp calls its savings figure "an estimate, not a guarantee" [voi 10].
