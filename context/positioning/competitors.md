## Where Ramp wins

| Where | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **One platform instead of a stack** | Studs: "We've consolidated four platforms into one with Ramp." [voi 1]. Ramp vs Corpay: "Corpay stitched together acquisitions that each kept their own codebase, login, and support team." [cmp 3] |
| **Control before spend, not after** | Ramp's Brex table lists "limited pre-spend controls" for Brex [cmp 3]; Ramp: "Build control into the card, before spend even happens." [voi 3.1] |
| **ERP depth** | Ramp claims real-time line-level sync with NetSuite, Sage and Oracle Fusion against Brex's journal entries [voi 9] |
| **Speed to switch** | Ramp "only takes days" [sig 1.3]; Coupa "6-12 months", Zip "8-plus-week setup" (Ramp's figures) [cmp 3]; Pair Eyewear: "The process took about a month." [cus 7] |
| **Ease of use** | G2 praise themes: ease of use (98 reviews) and receipt management (86) [cus 6.1]. Switchers cite usability or a dated interface most often (third-party study) [sig 7.2] |
| **Independent, not bank-owned** | Brex is now "a wholly owned subsidiary of Capital One"; Ramp says its roadmaps are "driven by finance team needs rather than broader banking consolidation" [sig 1.2] |

## Where Ramp loses

| Where | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Headline rewards rate** | Airwallex, Rho Platinum and Capital One Spark advertise up to 2%; Ramp's own FAQ says "up to 1.5%" [cmp 3, cmp 6]. A Brex reviewer: "The rewards were the main reason for us switching." [sig 2.10] |
| **Support** | Trustpilot 2.7 out of 5, 34% one-star, themes: support, vendor-payment delays [cus 6.4]. Brex's page: "Ramp customer service left them waiting weeks" (competitor marketing) [sig 2.10] |
| **Country coverage** | Brex: "in 120+ countries" [cmp 4.1]. Ramp is live for companies headquartered in the US, Canada and the UK only [com 4]. Brex's switchers wanted "more countries and currencies supported" [sig 2.10] |
| **Travel-first buyers** | Navan: 4.7 on G2 from 9K+ reviews and 45 Fortune 500 customers [cmp 4.3] |
| **Enterprise source-to-pay** | Coupa is a Gartner Leader for Source-to-Pay; Ramp's own page admits it [cmp 3, cmp 4.7] |
| **Keep-your-card buyers** | Expensify works with existing cards: "You don't have to switch corporate cards to use Expensify." [cmp 4.2] |
| **Perceived depth** | Numeral (on Brex's page): "Ramp felt designed around card management but not really spend management" [sig 2.10]. A CFO on TrustRadius: "too half baked for going all in on expense." [cus 6.6] |

## How to respond

| Objection or loss | Response (grounded in research) |
|---|---|
| "We earn more rewards elsewhere" | Move the conversation from points to total savings: Ramp says the median customer saves 7.5%, from controls and fewer tools, not only cashback [voi 10]. Ramp's Amex page: "customers often save more with Ramp than they ever did trying to game Amex rewards" [cmp 3]. Don't quote cashback rates in copy (facts.md ban) |
| "We've heard support is slow" | Point to onboarding: "Setup with a dedicated onboarding specialist." [sig 1.6]; Pair Eyewear: "I was blown away by Ramp's support as we onboarded." [cus 7] |
| "We operate in many countries" | For a US-headquartered company: cards accepted in 200+ countries, local issuing in 30+ countries on Enterprise, reimbursements in 40 currencies [com 2]. Notion: "Ramp is the only vendor that can service all of our employees across the globe in one unified system." [cus 3.1] |
| "We use Navan for travel" | Ramp Travel enforces policy at booking and rebooks hotels when the rate drops $50 or more [voi 3.5]; one platform instead of travel plus a separate expense suite [voi 9] |
| "We're evaluating Coupa" | Don't fight for enterprise source-to-pay. Target companies without a standing procurement team; Ramp's procurement setup is "About 3 weeks" against Zip's 8+ (Ramp's figures) [cmp 3] |
| "We'll keep our Amex and add a tool" | Show the cost of two systems: Eight Sleep "previously relied on American Express for corporate cards and Expensify" and moved both [cmp 3] |
| "It's a card, not spend management" | Lead with procurement, AP and accounting automation: Foursquare took spend under management from about 60% to nearly 100% [cus 3.10] |
| "What about Brex now that Capital One owns it?" | Don't use fear (facts.md ban; Ramp itself says Brex users "don't need to make a move yet") [sig 1.2]. Wait for real friction |
