# Ramp brand voice

Built only from `context/docs/research/voice.md` (checked 2026-11-14), section numbers in brackets. The tone descriptions are INFERRED from Ramp's own copy; the phrases are verbatim. For our own outbound copy, [messaging.md](../messaging/messaging.md) and `copy-bans.yaml` win where they differ (for example, Ramp uses "free" and "streamline"; our copy doesn't).

## Tone of voice (INFERRED from the copy)

| Trait | What it looks like | Ramp's own lines |
|---|---|---|
| **Short, paired lines** | Two or three short sentences or fragments in a row | "Time is money. Save both."; "Scale the team. Shrink the paperwork."; "Employees buy faster. Finance controls every dollar."; "No refreshing. No rechecking. Just savings." [5] |
| **Plain and confident** | Claims stated flat, often with a number | "Close your books 3x faster."; "A month of AP done in minutes." [3.3, 3.6] |
| **Against busywork, for time** | The enemy is manual work and stitched-together tools, not people | "Let agents handle the busywork."; "You had a bureaucracy. Now you have a business again."; "Stop stitching together five tools to run your finances." [1, 9] |
| **Finance-team first** | Speaks to finance teams, CFOs and controllers by name | "Keep your finance team focused on strategy." [1]; "Developed by CFOs and controllers" [6] |
| **Playful in brand moments** | Humour in ads and stunts, never in product claims | Super Bowl ad: "a purposefully absurd scene: show Saquon buried in a mountain of expense reports"; careers: "Locked "Brian in a box" with 600,000 receipts" [2, 4.4] |
| **Mission-led** | Everything ties back to saving time and money | "If it doesn't save you time or money, we don't build it." [2]; "Our mission hasn't changed. It's just gotten bigger." [2] |

## How Ramp names things

- Products are "Ramp" plus a plain noun: Ramp Intelligence, Ramp Bill Pay, Ramp Travel, Ramp Budgets, Ramp Stack, Ramp Sheets [6].
- AI features are "agents" with a job title: Policy Agent, Accounting Agent, "Auto-coding agent", "Approval agent" [6].
- The customer is "finance teams", "finance leaders", "businesses" or "teams"; employees are "Ramplings" [6].

## How Ramp talks about the old way

Ramp contrasts itself with "legacy" tools and manual work, not with named people: "2.4x faster than legacy software"; "Your team shouldn't have to play detective on every invoice."; "Cost overruns don't happen at month-end. That's just when finance finds them." [9]
