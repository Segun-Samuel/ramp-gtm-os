# Ramp messaging

Built only from `context/docs/research/` (checked 2026-11-14). Source codes: `cus` customers.md, `voi` voice.md, `sig` signals.md, `com` company.md. Claims usable in copy must also be in [facts.md](facts.md), which wins if the two disagree. INFERRED = my synthesis.

## Core message

**Ramp builds control into every dollar your company spends and closes the books automatically, so your finance team saves time and money.** (INFERRED synthesis of Ramp's own lines: "Time is money. Save both." and "One platform for all of finance." [voi 1])

## Message pillars

| Pillar | What it says | Proof points (Ramp claims) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Control before spend** | Policies live in the card, and an agent reviews every expense, so out-of-policy spend is stopped or flagged before month-end | Policy agent reviews "100% of your expenses" [voi 3.2]; "99% policy enforcement accuracy" (footnoted to Ramp product data) [voi 10]; Notion: 94% of transactions in policy and $1M+ a year saved from prevented out-of-policy spend [cus 3.1] |
| **2. A faster close with less manual work** | Receipts, memos and coding happen as spend happens, and sync to the ERP in real time | "Close your books 3x faster." [voi 3.6]; Perplexity: 97%+ auto-coded [cus 3.2]; Brandt: close 15 to 20 hours faster [cus 3.34]; median customer does 22% less manual work [voi 10] |
| **3. One platform instead of a stack** | Cards, expenses, bill pay, procurement, travel and accounting in one place, replacing tools that "never spoke" | "Systems that never spoke" [voi 1]; Studs: "We've consolidated four platforms into one with Ramp." [voi 1]; Foursquare: "cut our vendor stack by a third" [cus 3.10] |
| **4. Savings you can see** | Spend control plus fewer tools lowers total spend, not only rewards | Median customer saves 7.5% (an estimate, not a guarantee) [voi 10]; Foursquare: spend under management from about 60% to nearly 100% [cus 3.10] |

## Angle per persona

| Persona | Lead pillar | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Controller (champion) | 2 | Get receipts and codes as spend happens, so the close stops depending on chasing people. Audit evidence is collected at the moment of spend [sig 8] |
| CFO or VP Finance (economic buyer) | 1 and 3 | Control spend and scale the company without adding finance headcount (Brandt) [cus 3.34] |
| AP and accounting ops (influencer) | 2 | Less keying and fewer clicks: "A month of AP done in minutes." [voi 3.3] |

## Words to avoid

- Everything in `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml`, even where Ramp's own site uses it: em dashes, en dashes, "!", "free", "guarantee", "streamline", "seamless", "unlock", "empower", "game-changer", "curious".
- facts.md banned angles: diagnosing the prospect's team as failing, fear about Brex's new owner, disparaging competitors, price or cashback rates, the untraceable "70% of budget in 100 days" stat, any guarantee, implying the sender works for Ramp.
- Unattributed numbers: case-study metrics are Ramp's claims and need "per Ramp" or the customer's name.
