## Proof caveats

- Every case-study metric is Ramp's own claim on a Ramp page, and every quote was selected by Ramp. Attribute, never present as independent.
- "7.5%" is a median, and Ramp's footnote calls its savings figure "an estimate, not a guarantee". Never promise it to a prospect.
- Perplexity's "about 7,000 to 9,000 card transactions a month" came from a summarising fetch, not verbatim page text. Keep it out of copy until it is checked on the live page.
- Brandt's quoted people show no surnames on the page (Melissa M., Amy F.). Use first name and title only.
- Quotes must be re-checked on the live page before launch (customers.md method note).
- None of the four is a construction or healthcare proof; they support the software workflow only.

## Banned

### Banned angles (approved 2026-11-14)
- Diagnosing the prospect's team as failing ("your close is broken").
- Fear about competitors' ownership, e.g. "Brex is now a bank". Ramp itself says Brex users "don't need to make a move yet".
- Disparaging named competitors.
- Price, discounts or cashback rates.
- The "new executives spend 70% of budget in their first 100 days" stat (untraceable).
- Any guarantee of savings or time.
- Implying the sender works for Ramp.

### Banned words and phrasing (company-specific)
The global list is `knowledge-base/playbooks/copy-bans.yaml` (includes em dashes). Add only what is specific to this project: none yet.

### Never in copy, under any circumstance
- Review-site complaints and scores (Trustpilot, G2, TrustRadius).
- Churn and loss cases (Landry/French, Fyxer, Numeral, Chargeback).
- Anything from competitor marketing (Brex's pages).

## Voice notes (approved 2026-11-14)

- Register: direct and plain, short declarative lines, close to Ramp's own style ("Scale the team. Shrink the paperwork.", voice.md section 1).
- Hedges about the prospect: "my guess is", "could be off".
- Subjects: lowercase, 3 to 5 words, no variables.

## CTA ladder (approved 2026-11-14)

- Primary: offer the matching case study ("Worth sending the Brandt story?").
- Secondary (final email): ask who else owns spend tools.
- Never: calendar links or "book a call" in email 1.

## Internal only (never in prospect copy)

- Research gaps, review scores, ACV guesses, signal scores, reply rates and campaign performance.
