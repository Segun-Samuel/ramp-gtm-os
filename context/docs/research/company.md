Researched: 2026-11-14

Scope: Raw public-source facts about Ramp the company only (basics, products, pricing and revenue model, markets, funding and financials, team, leadership, other notable items). No conclusions or recommendations. Customers, competitors, market, signals and voice are covered by other files.

Method note: Pages were read through a summarising fetch tool, so text in quotation marks is only what the tool returned as verbatim quotes; everything else is a paraphrase. ## 1. Basics

- Name and entity: Ramp (Ramp Business Corporation, per a Digital Transactions news brief, "New York City-based payments and corporate card platform Ramp Business Corp."). (Source: https://www.digitaltransactions.net/ramp-acquires-juno-and-other-digital-transactions-news-briefs-from-3-16-26/, checked 2026-11-14)
- Founded: March 2019 per Wikipedia infobox; formally launched February 2020. Contrary Research: "In March 2019, Glyman and Atiyeh left Capital One to start Ramp." (Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramp_(company) and https://research.contrary.com/company/ramp, checked 2026-11-14)
- Founders: Eric Glyman (CEO, now Co-CEO), Karim Atiyeh (CTO, now Co-CEO), Gene Lee. Glyman and Atiyeh previously co-founded Paribus (2014), acquired by Capital One in 2016; Lee was a former Paribus engineer. (Source: https://research.contrary.com/company/ramp, checked 2026-11-14)
- HQ: New York City. Fortune (Sept 9, 2025) places it in the Flatiron district; Ramp's careers page footer lists "28 West 23rd Street, Floor 2" New York, NY 10010. (Source: https://fortune.com/article/ramp-american-express-corporate-credit-card-venture-capital-keith-rabois and https://ramp.com/careers, checked 2026-11-14)
- Ramp's own one-line descriptions (several versions on its own pages):
  - Homepage tagline: "Time is money. Save both." and "One platform for all of finance. Agents for every workflow, working 24/7." (Source: https://ramp.com/, checked 2026-11-14)
  - Mission line in the June 2026 Series F release (as reposted by GIC): "Ramp's mission is to save companies time and money." (Source: https://www.gic.com.sg/newsroom/all/ramp-raises-series-f-at-44-billion-valuation/, checked 2026-11-14)
  - Press page boilerplate (paraphrase, not verbatim): a finance operations platform combining corporate cards with software to manage all non-payroll spend (expenses, payments, accounting, reporting); customers "close their books 8 days faster each month" is a Ramp claim listed there. (Source: https://ramp.com/press, checked 2026-11-14)
  - Series F release boilerplate (paraphrase): financial infrastructure for card payments, invoices, reimbursements, approvals and automated bookkeeping, "from small family farms to Fortune 100 companies". (Source: https://otpp.com/en-ca/about-us/news-and-insights/2026/ramp-raises-series-f-at-44-billion-valuation/, checked 2026-11-14)
  - Ramp states it "is not a bank" (banking page: Ramp Business Corporation "is a financial technology company and is not a bank"). (Source: https://ramp.com/banking, checked 2026-11-14)

## 2. Products (what ramp.com says, with page URLs)

Navigation (Products menu on homepage): Corporate cards, Expense management, Spend management, Budgets, Banking, Travel, Reimbursements, Procurement, Accounts payable, Vendor management, Approvals, Security, Trust, Mobile app, Ramp Sheets. Platform menu: Platform overview, Accounting automation, Intelligence, Reporting, Savings, Integrations, Multi-entity, Global, AI Spend Management, Router. (Source: https://ramp.com/, checked 2026-11-14)

### Core spend products
- Corporate cards: headline "A corporate card designed for modern finance." Preset controls by vendor and category, custom virtual cards, approval workflows by amount or role, receipt capture by SMS, mobile app or integrations, rewards, "Over $350k in partner offers". Page cites acceptance in 200+ countries via Visa, local issuing in 30+ countries, reimbursement in 60+ countries and 40 currencies. (Source: https://ramp.com/corporate-cards, checked 2026-11-14)
- Expense management: "Automated expense management software built into your corporate card, reimbursements, and more." Spend controls, spend request templates, recommended actions on approvals, automatic receipt capture and memo/category fill, submission by app, SMS, Slack or Microsoft Teams, a policy agent that reviews every expense, ERP sync, natural-language spend questions, finance assistant chat. Reimbursements paid in 1-2 business days; accounting integration "about five minutes" per the FAQ. (Source: https://ramp.com/expense-management, checked 2026-11-14)
- Accounts payable (Bill Pay): "Process bills, accelerate approvals, and manage cash flow 2.4x faster than legacy software." AI invoice OCR "with 99% accuracy", auto-coding agent, 2-way and 3-way matching, approval routing, pay by card, ACH or wire, batching, 1099 filing, budget mapping. Page claims up to 7x fewer clicks than Bill.com (internal testing, Sept 2025). (Source: https://ramp.com/accounts-payable, checked 2026-11-14)
- Procurement: "AI agents handle purchasing from source to payment. Employees buy faster. Finance controls every dollar." Intake in plain language, vendor research and RFx generation, parallel approvals, AI contract and security reviews, renewals tracking, PO generation, 2-way and 3-way matching (also matches card transactions to POs). Claims: 16% median annual savings on vendor spend, 46 hours saved per month (includes planned features), 3x faster approvals. (Source: https://ramp.com/procurement, checked 2026-11-14)
- Travel: "Ramp enforces policy at booking, reconciles expenses automatically, and finds extra savings." Policy shown at booking, per diem, hotel rate monitoring with automatic rebooking when a rate drops $50 or more, works with any travel provider for flights, hotels and car rentals. (Source: https://ramp.com/travel, checked 2026-11-14)
- Reimbursements: "Pay back employees for out-of-pocket expenses made anywhere in the world." Page says "more than 70 countries and 40 currencies", payment within two days, mobile requests, Google Maps mileage. Note the corporate cards page says "60+ countries"; the two Ramp pages disagree. (Source: https://ramp.com/reimbursements and https://ramp.com/corporate-cards, checked 2026-11-14)
- Vendor management: "A single, holistic view of your vendors." Spend insights, contract extraction, renewal reminders at 60 and 30 days, custom fields, License Intelligence (Okta, listed as early access), Price Intelligence (benchmarks using crowd-sourced Ramp transaction data). (Source: https://ramp.com/vendor-management, checked 2026-11-14)
- Budgets: "Ramp Budgets tracks plan vs. actuals in real time across cards, reimbursements, Bill Pay, and committed POs." Tracks, does not enforce hard limits (per FAQ); only on Plus and Enterprise plans; nests up to five layers deep. (Source: https://ramp.com/budgets, checked 2026-11-14)
- Banking (Business Checking and Investment Account): "Business banking that works for you." Checking 2% APY "accurate as of 11/13/2026", variable; Investment Account "up to 4.73%" (1-day SEC yield or yield to maturity, net of advisory fee up to 0.15%, assumes $10M sample). Features: free same-day ACH and wires for Bill Pay, RTP, virtual accounts, 13-week cash flow forecasting. Page stats: 20,000+ companies "running cash on Ramp", $5B+ total customer balances as of 11/13/2026 (not regulatory AUM). (Source: https://ramp.com/banking, checked 2026-11-14)
- Ramp Treasury launched January 2025 (launch press coverage dated 2025-01-22). (Source: https://bradfordera.com/2025/01/22/ramp-launches-ramp-treasury-cash-management-that-maximizes-every-dollar-with-minimal-effort, checked 2026-11-14)
- Stablecoin accounts and payments: generally available to all customers July 21, 2026. Rewards "up to 3.25%" on average daily balance if a month's criteria are met ($5,000 deposit and five qualifying payments), stated not to be interest. US-based businesses except those in New York. Custody by Bridge Building Inc and affiliates. Three AI agents (invoice drafting, late-payment follow-up, cash application). "currently not available as a standalone product"; ERP support limited to QuickBooks Online and NetSuite; no CRM sync yet. (Source: https://ramp.com/accounts-receivable and https://www.prnewswire.com/news/ramp/, checked 2026-11-14)

## 3. Pricing model

### Plans (ramp.com/pricing, read 2026-11-14)
- Free: "$0/mo/user", "Best for smaller teams who want to simplify finances." Includes unlimited cards, expense management, AP payments (ACH, card, check, wire), basic accounting rules, QuickBooks Online and Xero integrations, report builder, vendor management, AR basics. (Source: https://ramp.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-14)
- Plus: "$15/mo/user" plus "Platform fee based on team size." and "Save 20% with annual billing". The platform fee amount is not shown. Adds NetSuite and Sage Intacct integrations, AI coding for every field, multi-entity, AI expense reviews, auto-lock cards when compliance is unmet, batch payments, AI approval recommendations, live report sharing, budget vs actuals, custom roles, audit log. Procurement is an add-on. 1099 filing listed at "$.65 per IRS filing. Free state filing." (Source: https://ramp.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-14)
- Enterprise: "Custom", annual billing. Adds implementation and enterprise services, Workday and Oracle Fusion Cloud integrations, local currency card issuing in 30+ countries, locally funded reimbursements, priority 24/7 global support, dedicated account and customer success manager. (Source: https://ramp.com/pricing, checked 2026-11-14)
- Plus trial and payment: FAQ says a "free 30-day trial"; Plus can be paid "with ACH or the rewards you earn from your Ramp card spending". Help centre says the free trial ends one month after sign-up. (Source: https://ramp.com/pricing and https://support.ramp.com/ramp-plus-billing-policy, checked 2026-11-14)
- Per-seat figure for annual billing: a third-party blog reports "$12 with annual billing" (INFERRED from the 20% discount: 15 x 0.8 = 12; Ramp's page does not print $12). (Source: https://www.corpay.com/resources/blog/ramp-pricing, checked 2026-11-14)
- Ramp Plus launched in 2023 (Fortune). (Source: https://fortune.com/article/ramp-american-express-corporate-credit-card-venture-capital-keith-rabois, checked 2026-11-14)
- Add-on / separate products: Router "Free through 2026"; AI Token Spend Management free to start (per coverage); Bill Pay users are also debited monthly Bill Pay fees (help centre, amounts not found). (Source: https://router.com, https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/16/ramp-targets-ais-fastest-growing-cost-expanded-token-spend-tracking/ and https://support.ramp.com/ramp-plus-billing-policy, checked 2026-11-14)

### Card terms
- Type: pricing page calls it a "Corporate liability charge card" with "Competitive credit limits" and "Unlimited physical and virtual cards." Corporate cards page: "Corporate charge card with 30-day payback"; no annual fee and no interest charges (FAQ). (Source: https://ramp.com/pricing and https://ramp.com/corporate-cards, checked 2026-11-14)
- No personal guarantee: "There's no personal credit checks or personal guarantee." Limit "based on financial factors such as revenue or dollars raised." (Source: https://ramp.com/corporate-cards, checked 2026-11-14)
- Credit limit method (US, Ramp help centre): "Ramp sets your card limit as a % of cash in your linked bank accounts" or via revenue-based underwriting; eligibility needs "at least $25,000 in cash" in a linked US bank account, US-registered corporation, LLC or LP. A third-party review (Makerstack, tested Mar 19, 2026) instead says applicants generally need "$75K or more in annual revenue"; the two sources differ. (Source: https://support.ramp.com/llms-full.txt and https://makerstack.co/reviews/ramp-review/, checked 2026-11-14)
- Cashback: ramp.com/corporate-cards and ramp.com/pricing do not print a cashback percentage. Fortune (Sept 9, 2025) reports "a flat 1.5% cash-back rate"; Makerstack (Mar 19, 2026) says 1.5% on all purchases with no category restrictions or caps. Ramp page title "Pricing Intelligence and 1.5% Cashback - Saving Insights" (ramp.com/real-savings). A Ramp blog (updated Aug 18, 2026) says "cashback is applied automatically as a statement credit, with no minimum thresholds or manual redemption required"; the corporate cards page says cashback can reduce the balance, be deposited into a business account, or be redeemed for travel and gift cards. (Source: https://fortune.com/article/ramp-american-express-corporate-credit-card-venture-capital-keith-rabois, https://makerstack.co/reviews/ramp-review/, https://ramp.com/blog/business-credit-cards-with-cashback, https://ramp.com/corporate-cards, checked 2026-11-14)
- Ramp's savings claim, not a cashback rate: "up to 7.5%" / "average savings of 7.5%" appears across Ramp pages, with a footnote that it is an estimated average and not a guarantee. (Source: https://ramp.com/corporate-cards, checked 2026-11-14)
- 1-day payment terms (daily payments) on the Ramp Card: payments pulled daily from Ramp Checking; limit "generally equal to 100% of the available balance in your Ramp Checking Account"; reported to credit bureaus; multi-entity companies cannot use 1-day terms. (Source: https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26807390083219-1-day-payment-terms, checked 2026-11-14)
- Issuing banks (US): "Ramp cards with 1-day terms are issued by Sutton Bank, Member FDIC"; Celtic Bank and Lead Bank also issue under their own terms (help centre). Contrary Research names Sutton Bank (Visa Commercial Card) and Celtic Bank (Visa Corporate Card). Canada: Ramp Visa Business Cards issued by Peoples Trust Company. UK: corporate cards issued by Stripe Payments UK Limited (FCA-authorised EMI). (Source: https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26807390083219-1-day-payment-terms, https://research.contrary.com/company/ramp, https://ramp.com/blog/introducing-ramp-in-canada, https://ramp.com/blog/uk-launch, checked 2026-11-14)
- Startup eligibility: card approval depends on cash balances, cash flow and revenue; EIN needed but "Having an EIN does not guarantee approval."; review may take one to two business days. Small business page: "Ramp underwrites your business based on its financial health—not your personal credit score." (Source: https://ramp.com/startups and https://ramp.com/small-business, checked 2026-11-14)
- Canada card eligibility: "at least CA$25,000" in connected accounts, no personal credit checks or guarantees (help centre). (Source: https://support.ramp.com/llms-full.txt, checked 2026-11-14)

### How Ramp makes money
- Ramp's own statement (help centre): interchange ("Every time you make a purchase, merchants incur a small fee known as an interchange fee"; Ramp receives a portion), Ramp Plus ("an optional paid subscription for advanced capabilities"), supplementary services such as international and same-day payments; "we do not profit from charging interest on carried balances." (Source: https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043060273-How-does-Ramp-make-money, checked 2026-11-14)
- Ramp's October 2026 description of annualized revenue: includes interchange, subscription and interest income (per release coverage; the PR Newswire/Morningstar page itself returned 403 so I relied on search summaries). (Source: https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20261015ny73055/ramp-reaches-60-billion-valuation-as-annualized-revenue-tops-2-billion, checked 2026-11-14)
- Fortune (Sept 9, 2025): "Per a person familiar with its finances, Ramp keeps about 0.8% of transaction volume" (Fortune's paraphrase); non-card revenue was under 5% of contribution profit when CFO Will Petrie joined and was expected to exceed 30% by end of 2025; Ramp reported positive cash flow in Q1 2025. (Source: https://fortune.com/article/ramp-american-express-corporate-credit-card-venture-capital-keith-rabois, checked 2026-11-14)
- Third-party estimate (ValueAdd VC, 2026, self-labelled "directional estimates, not disclosed audited figures"): about 70% card interchange, about 15% Ramp Plus seats, about 15% treasury yield and bill-pay fees combined. Not company-reported. (Source: https://valueaddvc.com/blog/how-does-ramp-make-money-card-interchange-software-fees-and-the-business-model-breakdown, checked 2026-11-14)
- Contrary Research: Ramp's actual take rate is not disclosed; its illustration is hypothetical (about 250 bps interchange, about 200 bps to issuing bank, about 50 bps to Ramp). Also names international bill payments, negotiations and Flex as other revenue sources. (Source: https://research.contrary.com/company/ramp, checked 2026-11-14)
- Banking and treasury: Ramp pays 2% on Checking and lists $5B+ customer balances (11/13/2026). INFERRED: the "interest income" in Ramp's revenue definition relates to balances like these; Ramp has not broken out the amount. (Source: https://ramp.com/banking, checked 2026-11-14)
- Float: Ramp states it is a charge card with 30-day payback; whether Ramp quantifies float income: Not found.
