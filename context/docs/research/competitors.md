# Ramp competitors: raw public-source research

Researched: 2026-11-14

Scope: Companies that sell to the same buyers as Ramp (finance leaders, controllers, CFOs, AP/AR and procurement teams, startups through enterprise): corporate card and spend platforms, expense and travel tools, AP automation, procurement, business banks, bank-issued cards and ERP-native tools. Raw facts only, no conclusions or recommendations. Other topics (company, customers, market, signals, voice) sit in other files.

## How to read this file

- Every fact ends with (Source: URL, checked 2026-11-14). First-pass quotes in quotation marks were copied from page text returned by a fetch tool that summarised pages, so punctuation such as dashes may differ slightly from the live page. Second-pass quotes (marked "second pass" or listed in section 8) were copied from full-page Markdown crawled with crawl4ai and are verbatim from that text.
- [SNIPPET] means the fact comes from a web search result summary and the underlying page was NOT opened. Treat as lower confidence until opened.
- INFERRED means my inference, not stated by the source.
- "Not found" means looked for and not sourced. Fetch failures are named in the section where they occurred and again in "Gaps and not found".
- Third-party figures carry their source and as-of date where one was available.
- Ramp's own claims about competitors are marketing statements by Ramp, not independent verification. Competitor claims about Ramp are marketing statements by that competitor.
